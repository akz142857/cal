# Open research roadmap

The roadmap is organized around falsifiable research questions rather than
feature volume.

## Near term

- Obtain independent reproduction of the published I1 result.
- Diagnose the L0 V8 permanence-control failure without reusing the consumed
  holdout as development data.
- Design a new externally held evaluation split and preregister its custody,
  metrics, controls, and release conditions.
- Reduce the public reproduction path to one documented command per stage.
- Publish versioned result bundles with checksums and archival DOI metadata.

## Environment generalization

- Add controlled observation noise and nuisance variation.
- Test novel layouts, entity counts, motion regimes, and camera geometry.
- Progress from binary occupancy to depth and RGB observations.
- Measure calibration and failure recovery under partial observability.

## Closed-loop world modeling

- Add action-conditioned future-state prediction over the entity belief graph.
- Compare explicit entity state against neural and object-centric baselines.
- Evaluate whether predicted states improve planning and control.
- Test long-horizon replanning after occlusion, identity ambiguity, and
  unexpected dynamics.

## Community projects

Good first research contributions include documentation replication,
additional negative controls, deterministic visualization improvements, and
baseline adapters. Larger proposals should begin in GitHub Discussions with a
short hypothesis and evaluation design.
