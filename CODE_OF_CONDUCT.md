# Community code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, evidence-driven, harassment-free community
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Be constructive. Critique claims, methods, evidence, and code rather than
people. State uncertainty, disclose conflicts of interest, credit prior work,
and treat negative results and failed replications as useful research
outcomes. Do not publish private information, credentials, or undisclosed
holdout material.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption,
and bad-faith manipulation of research evidence are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact method in `SECURITY.md`.
Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation,
or take other proportionate action to protect the community. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project
elsewhere.
